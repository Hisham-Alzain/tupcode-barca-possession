# Individual ball possession in soccer

- Authors: Daniel Link, Martin Hoernig
- Year / venue: 2017, PLOS ONE 12(7): e0179953
- URL: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0179953 ; PMC5503225

## Key method
Individual ball possession (IBP) from position data: a player's sphere of influence around the ball, using player-ball distance, direction, speed and ball acceleration; team possession derived from the IBP sequence. Validated against manually logged ground truth on TRACAB data.

## Data needed
Player AND ball positions (with IDs).

## Reported results
Concordance with manual logging reported; headline numbers not found in search snippets.
